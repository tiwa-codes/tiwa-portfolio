Nigerian Incident Intelligence Platform

Built a real-time incident detection and narrative intelligence platform for a government client. Aggregates and analyses news from Nigerian news sources using NLP: language detection, sentiment analysis, entity extraction, and classification across English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo.

  • ✦Multilingual NLP across English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo with fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa
  • ✦Geospatial heatmaps via PostGIS — bounding-box queries optimised from 800ms to 12ms
  • ✦AI-generated situation reports in under 30 seconds on demand
  • ✦Reduced analyst incident-review time by ~70% across 40+ continuously monitored sources

Claims, HR, finance, and compliance — one system, full audit trail

CrownJewel HMO: ERP System

Designed and built a full enterprise resource planning system for a Nigerian HMO. Covers claims processing with fraud detection, HR and attendance, financial transactions, provider registration and risk profiling, and underwriting, all behind a role-based access system with full audit trails.

  • ✦Six modules: Claims, HR & Attendance, Finance, Provider Registry, Underwriting, and Audit
  • ✦Rule-based fraud scoring on every claim — explainable to auditors, no black-box ML
  • ✦Role-based access enforced at the data layer via Prisma middleware, not just the UI
  • ✦Append-only audit trail covering 100% of financial transactions — hard compliance requirement

NTAL Telehealth

Built an inclusive, offline-first telehealth platform for community health workers in Nigeria. Designed for low-connectivity environments: accessible via USSD, SMS, and WhatsApp, with privacy-by-design principles and FHIR-compatible architecture.

  • ✦Three channels (USSD, SMS, WhatsApp) feeding a single backend — any phone, any network
  • ✦FHIR-compatible schema for future interoperability with DHIS2 and NHMIS
  • ✦Redis session state for USSD — sub-millisecond reads required for every menu step
  • ✦Offline-first PWA caches patient records and syncs automatically when connectivity returns
