MORE ABOUT ME

I'm Mariam, a creative engineer

I started coding in 2019, and since then I've built full-stack web apps, AI-powered systems, and data pipelines — from architecture to deployment. Based in Abuja, I've delivered projects for healthcare, government, and social impact clients across Nigeria — including startups like Campus Collab, Atara Space, and NextGen Dialogues — and I'm open to remote work globally.

I care deeply about writing code that is readable, scalable, and maintainable — and about building products that actually solve real problems for real people.

Outside of code, I like bringing structure to creative chaos — organising systems in Notion, writing about tech on the side, and shooting photography that quietly feeds back into how I think about design.
